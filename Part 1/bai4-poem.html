I LOVE LITTLE KITTY

I love little kitty
Her coat is so warm.
And if I don't hurt her
She'll do me no harm.
I won't pull her tail,
Or drive her away
And kitty and I
Very gently will play.

THE CAT AND THE FIDDLE

Hey, diddle, diddle!
The cat and the fiddle,
The cow jumped over the moon.

THE CATS WENT OUT TO SERENADE

The cats went out to serenade
Beneath the silver moon.
